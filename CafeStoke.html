Privacy Policy

CafeStock · Last updated: November 21, 2026

CafeStock (“the app”) is an offline inventory manager for coffee shops. This policy explains how the app handles information.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information or usage data. The app does not require an account.

2. Data stored on your device

Information you enter (such as product names, quantities, prices, suppliers, notes and stock history) and your settings are stored locally on your device only. This data is never sent to us or to any third party. We cannot access it.

3. Internet access and permissions

The app works fully offline. It does not request internet access or any other Android permissions.

4. Third-party services

The app does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, or any third-party SDKs.

5. Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can erase all data at any time using “Clear application data” in Settings. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android settings also deletes all data permanently.

6. Children’s privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
